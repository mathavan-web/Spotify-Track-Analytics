Key Findings

• Energy & danceability are positively associated with popularity. High-tier tracks average +0.081 energy and +0.054 danceability over Low-tier tracks.
• Instrumentalness & acousticness are negatively associated with popularity. Down 0.177 and 0.115 respectively — This pattern is descriptive and does not establish causation.
• Tempo has almost no linear relationship (r = 0.02); the 140–160 BPM band edges out other ranges only slightly.
• Minor-key tracks average marginally higher popularity than Major-key tracks (22.3 vs 21.6), a very small gap.
• Some less-common genre combinations top the popularity leaderboard once a 100-track minimum filters out one-off outliers — reggaetón, corridos and hip-hop/rap variants dominate.
